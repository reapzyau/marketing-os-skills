# Chapter 2 — Attraction Offers (Turn Strangers Into Customers)

## Hormozi's why
"How to turn eyeballs into money." Attraction Offers generate leads and convert them to customers — usually via something free or discounted — ideally pulling enough up-front cash to cover the customer and the cost to deliver several times over. Free, discount, and $1 sit on one continuum and are interchangeable.

## Framework — the plays (pick ONE for now)

**1. Win Your Money Back** — *"Do X within Y time under Z rules → get it free."* Customer pays; wins it back (cash or store credit) if they hit the results/actions. Best for things people start and quit (fitness, skills, business). Criteria must be (1) easy to track, (2) drive results, (3) advertise the business. Apply winnings as **store credit toward a bigger/longer package**. ~10% ask for cash back — only run if refund rate <5%. ($1B+ industry-wide.)

**2. Giveaways** — *"Many enter, many win."* Advertise a chance to win a big prize for contact info; one Grand Prize winner, then everyone else gets it at a "partial scholarship" discount. Grand Prize = the thing you want everyone to buy, with a $ value anchor. Discount ≈ 10–30% of gross margins. "If your giveaway doesn't work, your Grand Prize wasn't grand enough."

**3. Decoy Offer** — *"Which will get you the best results?"* Advertise a free/discounted lesser version; when they engage, show the decoy AND a far-better premium side by side, emphasizing premium. (Gym: 8 of 10 took the $399 premium.) Make the contrast huge; present premium first; assumed close.

**4. Buy X Get Y Free** — *"Buy one, get two free."* Customers who buy get extra stuff free; reframes a discount as a stronger free offer. **Raise prices before giving stuff away.** Give more free than paid ("Buy 1 Get 2," not "Buy 2 Get 1"). Manage the cash (you may collect months of payments at once — deliver).

**5. Pay Less Now or Pay More Later** — *"Time is money."* A choice: full price later (card on file + conditional satisfaction guarantee) OR a discounted price now (20–50% off + bonuses). Combines delayed payment + guarantee = removes all risk. Promise a clear, measurable yes/no result. For one-time offers (vs Trial With Penalty for recurring).

**6. Free Goodwill** — a reciprocity play: give genuine value/goodwill publicly to earn trust + word of mouth (the book's own "leave a review" ask; the pizzeria foodbank giveaway). Not a hard sell — a long-game trust builder.

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
**Q (AskUserQuestion):** "Which attraction play fits your offer best right now?" — Win Your Money Back / Giveaway / Decoy / Buy-X-Get-Y / Pay-Less-Now / Free Goodwill (with trade-offs).
Plus:
- "Does your result require ongoing customer effort (→ Win Your Money Back) or is it one-and-done?"
- "Do you want max leads (free) or higher show-up (discount)?"
- "What's your most desirable product to anchor as the Grand Prize / premium?"
- "Do you have something to sell NEXT to apply the winnings/credit to?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- ONE play chosen, matched to the business, with trackable terms + a value anchor + deadlines
- A card-on-file step and a defined NEXT offer (winnings/credit have somewhere to go)
- Refund tolerance acknowledged (<5%) where relevant

Push back if:
- Giving away free with nothing to sell next
- Un-grand grand prize / faked value anchor
- Not raising prices before Buy-X-Get-Y; over-promising on Pay Later
- Trying to run all six at once

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- Free with nothing to sell next; un-grand grand prizes; faked anchors
- Not raising prices before giving stuff away; over-promising on Pay Later
- Running giveaways without legal counsel (illegal lotteries); refusing legitimate refunds ("blood money")
