# Chapter 5 — Post Free Content

> Build in depth ONLY if Post Free Content was chosen as the primary channel in Ch 3. Otherwise note it as a deferred channel and move on.

## Hormozi's why
Posting free content (1-to-many to a warm audience) lets you say something once and reach many — far more engaged leads per unit of time than warm outreach. The content isn't the compounding asset — **the audience is.** And free content makes every other Core Four method more effective.

## Framework

**Part I — make audience-growing content. The Content Unit = hook + retain + reward:**
- **Hook** (get noticed): **Topics** (Far Past / Recent Past / Present / Trending / Manufactured — prefer "How I" over "How to"); **Headlines** (include ≥2 news components: Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity, Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing); **Format** (match the best-performing content on the platform).
- **Retain** (get consumed): drive **curiosity** via Lists, Steps, Stories (interweave freely). "There's no such thing as too long, only too boring."
- **Reward** (satisfy the why): value per second; if the audience grows, you did good.
- Short vs long form = just link content units together. Start small, then build.

**Part II — monetise the audience:**
- **Give:Ask ratio** — "jab jab jab right hook." Mature platforms run ~3.5:1; growing platforms massively over-give. Hormozi: **"Give until they ask"** — give so much people come to you (give in public, ask in private; lets the audience self-select; never slows growth).
- **How to ask:** **Integrated** (an ask in most pieces — best for long-form) or **Intermittent** (e.g. 10 give : 1 ask — best for short-form). Advertise the **core offer OR the lead magnet** — default the lead magnet (lower risk).
- **Scale:** **Depth-then-width** (max one platform, then add) or **Width-then-depth** (all platforms early, then max together). Pick one.
- **Integrate with warm outreach** — content grows the warm audience, which feeds warm outreach. Do both.

**7 lessons:** "How to"→"How I"; remind > teach (repeat yourself); Puddles→Ponds→Lakes→Oceans (narrow first); content arms salespeople; free content retains buyers; higher standards not shorter attention; avoid pre-scheduling. **Benchmark:** 78% of clients consumed ≥2 long-form pieces before booking a call.

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
1. "What personal experience/topic can only YOU tell (which topic bucket)?"
2. "Does your headline include ≥2 news components?"
3. "How are you keeping people curious — lists, steps, or stories?"
4. "What's your give:ask ratio — are you over-giving enough to grow?"
5. (AskUserQuestion) "Scale by depth-then-width or width-then-depth?"
6. "What's your fixed posting cadence (and will you not stop)?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- Content built as hook + retain + reward, with a real curiosity device
- A give-heavy ratio + a chosen ask strategy + the lead magnet/offer to advertise
- A scaling approach + a fixed cadence + monthly measurement (total reach + growth rate)

Push back if:
- "How to" / preachy framing; asking too often (kills growth — "don't kill the golden goose")
- No curiosity device; making "sucky fluff"
- Stopping early — compounding needs time

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- Asking too frequently (shrinks the audience)
- "Sucky fluff everywhere"; local businesses making generic content too early
- Pre-scheduling posts; quitting before compounding kicks in
