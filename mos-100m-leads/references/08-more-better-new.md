# Chapter 8 — Core Four on Steroids (More, Better, New)

## Hormozi's why
Most owners plateau on the "Size of the Pie Fallacy" — assuming the slice they advertise to is the whole market. There are more leads than you can imagine. Three levers scale ANY channel: do MORE, do it BETTER, add NEW — in that order.

## Framework
- **MORE (do first):** even with no improvements, double inputs → more engaged leads. **The Rule of 100:** 100 primary actions/day for 100 days straight. Warm/Cold = 100 reach-outs/day; Content = 100 minutes/day (release ≥1/day); Paid Ads = 100 minutes/day + run them 100 days straight at the calculated budget (aim for client-financed acquisition).
- **BETTER (testing):** do more until it "breaks," then fix the **constraint** — the step with the biggest drop-off (most bang per fix). Test **one thing per platform per week**; log every result; if you can't beat your best in 4 tries / 1 month, move to the next constraint. (Improving a 5%-apply constraint by 5 points ≈ 2× leads — far more than tweaking a step that's already strong.)
- **NEW (last):** only once More/Better returns drop below what a new placement would give. Order: **New Placements → New Platforms → New Core Four activity** (pick by most leads for the work).

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
1. "What's stopping you from doing 10× the volume you do now?"
2. "Which single step has the biggest drop-off (your constraint)?"
3. "Are you doing the Rule of 100 on your chosen channel?"
4. "Have you exhausted More + Better before reaching for New?"
5. (AskUserQuestion) "Next New: a placement, a platform, or a new Core Four activity?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- A clear 10× volume plan (Rule of 100 / Open To Goal)
- An identified constraint + one test per platform per week, logged
- New only after More/Better are maxed

Push back if:
- "I've saturated my market" (the fallacy); testing many things at once (can't tell what worked)
- Jumping to New before maxing volume; wasting tests on trivial changes; not tracking throughput

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- The "saturated my niche" excuse; testing multiple things at once
- Reaching for New before exhausting More/Better; trivial tests (red → bright red); not tracking throughput

## Workbook output expected
A scale plan for the chosen channel: a Rule-of-100 volume commitment, the named constraint + a weekly testing cadence, and the next New expansion gated behind maxing More/Better.
