# Chapter 6 — Cold Outreach

> Build in depth ONLY if Cold Outreach was chosen as the primary channel in Ch 3. Otherwise note it as a deferred channel and move on.

## Hormozi's why
Cold outreach (1-to-1 to strangers) makes lead flow faster and more predictable, no longer capped by your warm audience — and uniquely, *you pick your targets*. It's a numbers game: more reach-outs → more engaged leads. But it takes longer than you think.

## Framework — strangers create 3 problems, solved in order

**Problem #1 — Build a list (3 sources, easiest → hardest):**
1. **Software** scrapers — test a few hundred from each, keep what's accurate/responsive.
2. **Brokers** — use multiple, build to your criteria, test samples.
3. **Elbow grease** — manually scrape from groups/directories (freshest leads — least contacted by competitors).
Goal: assemble your first **1,000 names**.

**Problem #2 — Personalise, then Big Fast Value:**
- **a) Personalise** — 1-3 facts a friend would know; compliment + show how it benefited you. "Make a cold reach-out look like a warm reach-out."
- **b) Big Fast Value** — strangers give you <30 seconds; "blow their minds in under thirty seconds." Give away something people *actually pay for* (core offer, lead magnet, or both).

**Problem #3 — Volume (3 levers):**
- **a) Automate delivery** (pre-recorded voice/video, templated email/text).
- **b) Automate distribution** (dialers, blasts — trades personalisation for scale; personalise more for high-value targets, less for broad ones).
- **c) Follow up — more times, more ways** (multi-touch, multi-channel; "act like you're actually trying to get hold of them"). After exhausting the list, **wait 3-6 months and run it again.**

**Activity / benchmarks:** Phone — 100 calls/day, 20% pickup, 25% take = ~4 engaged/day. Email — 100/day, 30% open × 10% reply ≈ 3% engaged (aim for 3%). DM — 100 personal video/voice, ~20% reply = 20 engaged, then A-C-A. Rule: a customer's lifetime profit ≥ **3× cost to acquire** (bare minimum).

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
1. "Exactly which strangers do you want (the more specific, the better)?"
2. (AskUserQuestion) "How will you build your first 1,000-name list — software / brokers / elbow grease?"
3. "What 1-3 personalisation points can you find per lead?"
4. "Is your 'big fast value' something people *actually pay for*?"
5. "How many touches, across how many channels, before you give up?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- A precise target profile + a real list-building method
- Genuine big-fast-value (pay-for-it quality), not "tickle their interest"
- Multi-touch, multi-channel follow-up + a re-run plan; metrics tracked

Push back if:
- Vague targets; no list; mediocre value
- One-and-done outreach; expecting results in weeks not months; no tracking

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- Underestimating volume AND time (it took Hormozi ~a year, not 12 weeks)
- Mediocre value that blends into the ocean; not following up
- Not tracking metrics (his two prior failures never tracked); quitting after a few months
