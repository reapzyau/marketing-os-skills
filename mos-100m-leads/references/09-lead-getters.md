# Chapter 9 — Lead Getters (The Four Horsemen)

## Hormozi's why
"The last skill you ever need is how to get other people to do everything for you." Once your own Core Four channel works, you get OTHER people to get leads for you — customers (referrals), employees, agencies, and affiliates. This completes the $100M leads machine. Pick which to add FIRST (usually referrals) and the trigger for the rest. Don't build all four before your chosen channel is working.

## Framework — the Four Horsemen

### 1. Customer Referrals — Word of Mouth (usually first)
Lowest-cost, highest-profit, best-quality leads — and **exponential**. **Referral growth = Referrals in − Churn out** (if referrals > churn, you grow with zero other advertising). Two reasons people don't get them: the product isn't as good as they think, or they don't ask. **Build goodwill** (value − price) via better-fit customers, better expectations, better/faster results, continuous improvement. **Then ASK — treat it like an offer.** Seven ways: one-sided benefit, two-sided benefit (Dropbox/PayPal), ask right when they buy, referral as a negotiation chip, referral events, ongoing programs, unlockable bonuses.

### 2. Employees ("the most valuable chapter")
Hire people to do the Core Four for you. Leverage: ~40 hrs *doing* → ~4 hrs *managing*. A business that runs without you is a sellable ASSET, not a job. Source via the **Internal Core Four** (ask your network / recruit / job posts / promoted posts / employee referrals). Train via the **3 Ds: Document → Demonstrate → Duplicate.** Train them to follow directions (then bad results = bad directions, which you control).

### 3. Agencies (use them to LEARN, then bring in-house)
Not to outsource forever — to learn **new methods + new platforms**, then internalise. Avoid the senior→junior bait-and-switch. Use the "work with you for 6 months, pay extra to learn *why*, then move to a lower-cost consulting arrangement" agreement. Run your own team in parallel; switch to discounted consulting once yours wins, then cut them loose. Pick on the 10 signs (esp. word-of-mouth reputation — good agencies are expensive, but not all expensive agencies are good).

### 4. Affiliates & Partners (highest leverage; "made me the most money")
Independent businesses that advertise your stuff to their own audiences. **Six steps:** find ideal affiliates ("Who's got my leads?") → make them a money-making offer → qualify (make them a customer + an expert; charge 10-20% of year-one earnings) → set a **three-tier payout** from your max allowable CAC → launch with **Whisper → Tease → Shout** → integrate (they give away your lead magnet / sell your lead magnet / sell your core offer). Pay forever; never cap.

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
**Q1 (AskUserQuestion):** "Which lead-getter do you add FIRST?"
Options: **Customer Referrals** (highest quality — do first), **Employees** (free your time), **Agencies** (learn a new method/platform fast), **Affiliates** (highest leverage, needs a system).
**Q2 (referrals):** "What's your referral % vs churn %? Do you actually ask — and is it framed as an offer?"
**Q3:** "What's the trigger to add the next Horseman (after N customers / first hire / channel maxed)?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- ONE Horseman chosen to add first, with a concrete mechanism (e.g. a two-sided referral offer)
- A trigger defined for adding the next
- For referrals: referral% > churn%, a genuinely remarkable product, an explicit ask framed as an offer

Push back if:
- Trying to build all four at once before the chosen Core Four channel works
- "Everyone loves us, we just need word out" but they never ask
- Leaky bucket (churn ≥ referrals); uninvested affiliates; hiring with no 3-Ds training

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- Building all four before the chosen Core Four channel works
- Referrals: believing the product is better than it is; never asking; leaky bucket
- Employees: "no one can do it like me"; hiring without 3-Ds training; unsellable owner-dependent business
- Agencies: staying dependent; choosing on price; falling for the senior→junior bait-and-switch
- Affiliates: uninvested affiliates; capping payouts; "affiliates can't work for my business"

## Workbook output expected
The first Horseman to add + its mechanism, a referral baseline if relevant, a trigger for the next, and a rough year sequence. Lead-getters scale the machine AFTER the chosen Core Four channel is working.
