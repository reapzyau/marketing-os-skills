# Chapter 4 — Warm Outreach

> Build this chapter in depth ONLY if Warm Outreach was chosen as the primary channel in Ch 3. Otherwise note it as a deferred channel (with a trigger to add later) and move on.

## Hormozi's why
Warm outreach (1-to-1 to people who know you) is the cheapest, easiest, most reliable way to find interested people — "as certain as the sun rises." Everyone has a list, even if they don't realise it. It's how you get your First Five Clients.

## Framework — the 10 steps
1. **Get your list** — pull every contact from phone, all email accounts, all social platforms. Often your first 1,000 leads.
2. **Pick a platform** — the one with the most contacts; you'll hit them all eventually.
3. **Personalise** — use something you know about them. "Don't be a weirdo, pay your social dues."
4. **Reach out to 100 people every day** — up to 3 touches (once/day for 3 days, or until they reply).
5. **Warm them up — A-C-A:** **Acknowledge** (restate in your words) → **Compliment** (tie to a positive trait) → **Ask another question** (steer toward the offer).
6. **Invite their friends — make the offer** (after ~3-4 exchanges). Built on the Value Equation. Don't ask them to buy — ask: "Do you know anybody who is [struggle] looking to [dream outcome] in [time]? I'm taking on five case studies for free…" then "anyone you *hate* come to mind?" to break tension.
7. **Make the easiest offer in the world — FREE** (first five free) if they (1) use it, (2) give feedback, (3) leave a review. On a "no," ask "why?" / "what would make it worth it?" to expose hidden costs.
8. **Start at the top** — exhaust one platform, then move to the next-biggest.
9. **Start charging** — once people *refer*, that's your litmus test. Swap "free" for "80% off for the next five," then 60/40/20%, raising 20% each five until your sweet spot (adds urgency too).
10. **Keep the list warm** — give regular value; probe with Dean Jackson's **9-word email**: "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?" (no links, no frills).

**Activity math:** ~1 in 5 contacts engage; ~1 in 5 of those take the free offer; ~1 of those 4 converts → ~1 customer per 100 reach-outs. $104k/yr example: 500 reach-outs/week → 5 customers/week × $400 product = $2,000/wk × 52 = $104,000.

## Diagnostic questions (ask the user)
1. "How many total contacts across phone, email, and socials?"
2. "Which platform holds the most?"
3. "Can you commit to 100 personalised reach-outs/day (not 100 over weeks)?"
4. "What's your A-C-A flow + your 'do you know anybody' offer script?"
5. "Has anyone referred you yet — your signal to start charging?"

## Validation criteria (what "good" looks like)
- A concrete list count + a daily 100-reach-out commitment
- A written value-equation offer (the "five free case studies" ask)
- A plan to give free first, ask "why?" on every no, and start charging at the referral signal

Push back if:
- "I don't have any leads" — everyone has a list
- 100 spread over weeks instead of per day
- Asking for the sale directly instead of "do you know anybody"
- Charging before earning referrals; quitting after a few nos

## Pitfalls (Hormozi's warnings)
- "I don't have any leads"; doing 100 over time, not per day
- Being pushy / asking for the buy directly; quitting after nos ("Nos give me feedback")
- Not personalising; charging too early
